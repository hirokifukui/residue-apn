# TRUST — what is checked by whom, and what is not

## Labels
- **F** — a Lean 4 statement, identified by its declaration name, proved and checked by the Lean kernel; axioms a
  subset of propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound; no sorry.
- **M** — proved in the paper only (checked by reading, not by the kernel).
- **C** — a finite computation over a stated range; never a proof of a general statement.

## Coverage
Item by item in `blueprint/blueprint.pdf`, generated by `blueprint/gen_blueprint.py` from `blueprint/nodes.json`. The
generator checks that each named Lean declaration is audited in `supplement/lean/Audit.lean` and defined in exactly one
module (`blueprint/check.txt`); it does not check the types against the paper. Most results are proved for an
abstract finite ring with a surjection onto a finite field (kernel `2R`, `2^k = 0`, 2-torsion `2^{k-1}R`); the
declarations `grs`, `grs_card` and `exists_GR_poly` prove that `GR(2^k, m)` satisfies these axioms. Difference counts
in Lean are cardinalities of actual finite solution sets.

## Trust boundary
The meaning of the Lean definitions, the Lean kernel, the pinned Mathlib and the toolchain are trusted. The finite
computations trust gcc, Python, SymPy and NumPy. Proofs labelled M are checked by reading only. Nothing here is
journal peer review.
