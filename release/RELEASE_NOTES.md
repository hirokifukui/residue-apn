**residue-apn v2.0.0** — Coefficient choice in Galois-ring lifts of APN permutations: sharp unit-direction spectra and one-term optimal lifts.

A new, substantially revised and extended paper replaces the paper of v1.0.x (Explicit reduced APN permutations: ramification and optimal Galois-ring lifts). The package is rebuilt around it: `paper/`, `blueprint/` (new), `supplement/` (Lean 4 development — 55 modules, 150 audited declarations with axioms propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound and no sorry — and the finite computations with replay scripts and negative controls). The material of v1.0.x remains under the tags `v1.0.0` and `v1.0.1` and in their Zenodo versions.
